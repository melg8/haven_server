How to play

Create a match and share the invite ID or link with your friend — the battle starts the moment they join. Click one of your soldiers to select it (green dots = possible steps), click a dot to queue a move; it resolves on a probabilistic dice roll every tick. Right-click deselects. Pawns that touch enemies lock into melee on their own; reach the far rank to upgrade into artillery.
